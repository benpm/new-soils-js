# Concepts

# Core Philosophy and Motivation

- Minecraft's current design disproportionately rewards technical players and automated mob grinders while punishing casual players who prefer building, exploring, and treating the world organically
- New Soils core design should revolve around exploration, learning the dangers of the environment and preparing for them by being resourceful.

# Hunger and Healing

- No hunger bar
- Players must consume food to heal, but are granted infinite sprinting capabilities.
- **Intrinsics:** Potion effects are offloaded to cooking. Ingredients have hidden abilities ("intrinsics") awakened by cooking (e.g., grilled pumpkin grants resistance)
- Animal flesh has no special properties, incentivizing crop cultivation over animal slaughterhouses.
- You drop a gravestone upon death. It can be opened for a crystal heart by a different player. The higher value your inventory, the more it costs.
- You can break your gravestone to get your stuff back. If a player buys your stuff with crystal hearts, you immediatly get those crystal hearts. These hearts are special. They are Bleak Crystal Hearts and they can have effects ***(TBD...)***
- Instead, players collect and craft "Crystal Hearts" which increase max health. No limit, 
- Upon death, the player loses his inventory unless he chooses to keep it by spending a crystal heart


# Villager and Economy Rebalance

- Villagers no longer buy items from players, breaking the loop of automated farms generating infinite emeralds (renamed to "Oals" in this pack)
- Fishermen are the sole exception and will pay handsomely for items, eV  couraging manual fishing.
- **Warding Stones:** New craftable items that protect bases by burning nearby undead and healing villagers

# Mob and Combat Scaling

- **Stat adjustments:** Zombies and cave spiders are significantly faster. Skeletons, creepers, and cave spiders have reduced maximum health
- **Spawn mapping:** Undead only spawn on the surface; creepers and spiders are restricted to caves and forests.
- **Drops:** Rotten flesh is replaced with "tattered leather" and "raw Estus" (which heals or can be converted to magical Estus Ash).
- **Post-Dragon Peace:** Defeating the Ender Dragon stops hostile mobs (zombies, skeletons, creepers) from spawning on the surface world, rewarding the player with uninterrupted building conditions

# Early Game Quality of Life & Tools

- **Inventory:** Shulker boxes are craftable in the early game to mitigate inventory management friction
- **Hybrid Tools:** Added "Delabbras" (pickaxe/axe hybrid) and "Maddox" (shovel/hoe hybrid) which save hotbar space but have slower base mining speeds
- **Tool Repair:** The XP repairing cap is removed. Tools can be repaired indefinitely using their base metal components

# New Alloys and Armor Systems
- Four new metals are introduced, each with specific buffs and intrinsic properties
- **Hepatitis (Gold/Silver/Copper):** High mining speed; increases running speed when worn.
- **Shikudo (Gold/Copper):** Grants lifesteal and cleanses malifacts; tools possess intrinsic Silk Touch.
- **Steel (Processed Iron):** Highly durable, knockback-heavy, and blast-resistant.
- **Electrum (Silver/Gold):** Grants maximum Looting/Fortune; burns nearby undead when held or worn.

# Enchanting and Progression

- RNG-based enchanting and XP costs are completely removed
- Enchantments (Efficiency, Unbreaking, Mending, etc.) are now explicitly crafted using rare materials like Estus Ash, Benzene, and Divine Gifts.
- **Divine Fragments/Favors:** Looted from spawners or powerful bosses (Dragon, Wither). These items act as bottlenecks for high-tier crafting, deliberately shifting the gameplay loop away from AFK mob grinders toward active dungeon clearing and boss fights
