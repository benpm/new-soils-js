# Story

- You are a Bulk Privateer: a wandering acolyte of the Bulk Seekers, tasked with seeking knowledge of the beings who populated an ancient city that contains an entire universe of connected worlds
- Your task is to explore this Bulk (the universe the ancient people either discovered or created), and try to maintain a supply line leading back to your home world
- To progress, you must find and activate the unique portal that was left behind in every world
- Throughout the game you do not know whether these beings are still alive, but eventually you do discover their technology is still functioning
    - After that, you discover that you are being watched by some entity that has travelled this path before you, and is trying for some reason to cover its tracks
    - Eventually, this entity makes itself known to you - it is yourself, or a copy of yourself, that has travelled through time (or you have travelled through time, and this copy experiences time at a slower rate to prevent you from catching up. You do not know why at first)

- Progression requires you to learn about the way the ecosystems of the many connected worlds function, what the wildlife and other intelligent beings are capable of, and their motives
    - Giant worms in desert worlds
- Each world has unique biomes, and a boundary, of which there are multiple kinds
    - Finite space boundary - the world loops in all directions. The tell-tale sign of these is that it is always night, no stars, only massive glowing crystals growing downards from the ceiling. As you go deeper, you will eventually fall down to the earth.

## Manifold
The Manifold is a liminal space that connects worlds, kind of like a 3D map that slowly changes with time. You travel through it in an Extrusion Vehicle - different vehicles can traverse the 3D.

### Death in the Manifold
Death in the Manifold is a much more terminal kind of death. You cannot die in a traditional sense, but you can become trapped in a Temporal Vortex - a time trap that causes your projection to be become eternally frozen. Any attempts to retrieve your projection will be futile - so consider your inventory forfeit.

## Bulk
The Bulk is composed of Worlds that can be explored, traveled to, and studied. Due to the inherant danger of this kind of work, your mortal flesh is not put directly at risk. Your physical presence is merely projected out into the Bulk via the Puppeteer Mechanism - an Enda Artifice

## Ebb
Ebb is the destabiling force that causes world decay. Regions of the Manifold vary in Ebb, so worlds embedded within those regions will Ebb quicker or slower, with true stability (zero Ebb) only being acheivable naturally. Ebb cannot be reversed, but it can be stopped via Xor Artifice.
